## Lead — Kyiv's bridges hit overnight (Ukraine)

Overnight on 19–20 November, Russia fired one of the largest missile and
drone barrages of the war at Ukraine: Kyiv reported about 110 missiles and
about 400 drones over more than 10 hours. Three bridges across the Dnipro
in Kyiv — Podilskyi, Darnytskyi and Pivdennyi — were damaged and traffic
restricted. Eight people were killed (six in Kyiv) and 28 wounded across
nine regions; power infrastructure was hit and outages followed. The
strikes were retaliation for Ukraine's attacks on the Kerch Bridge.
Sources: Reuters, BBC, The Times (20 Nov 2026).

## Card 1 — Russian missiles cross into Poland

During the same overnight barrage, three Russian objects entered Polish
airspace in the south-east: a Kh-101 cruise missile came down near Nowy
Lipowiec, a second missile at Rudnik nad Sanem, and a jet drone at
Molodycz. There were no casualties and no critical infrastructure damage.
Poland called it a deliberate provocation and asked NATO Secretary General
Mark Rutte for more surveillance and forces for Operation Eastern Sentry;
Rutte condemned a "grave violation" and NATO jets were scrambled.
Sources: Reuters, The Times (20 Nov 2026).

## Card 2 — UK–EU reset sealed in Brussels

Britain and the European Union agreed a post-Brexit reset package at a
Brussels summit on 20 November: a youth mobility scheme of 100,000 people
a year in each direction, ages 18–31, for stays of up to 3 years, in force
by January 2030 at the latest; a food and plant trade (SPS) deal removing
certificates and checks from March 2028, which Britain estimates is worth
£5.1 billion a year; and linked emissions-trading systems. EU student
tuition fees in Britain were left unresolved, with a six-month deadline.
Prime Minister Andy Burnham called it a "landmark" moment.
Sources: Reuters, CNN, BBC (20 Nov 2026).

## Card 3 — Saudi intercepts Houthi missile and drones

On Friday morning the Saudi-led coalition said air defences intercepted
and destroyed a Houthi ballistic missile and drones aimed at Saudi Arabia,
including Riyadh and the southern region. Air-raid warnings were issued
for Riyadh, Jazan, Abha, Khamis Mushait and Najran, then lifted. It came
less than a day after a Houthi strike on Thursday damaged the PIF Tower
in Riyadh's financial district. No drone count is given on the page
because reports differed.

## Card 5 — Rikers Island to close in 2032 (New York)

New York Mayor Zohran Mamdani announced on Friday a plan to close the
Rikers Island jail complex in 2032, proposing a five-year delay to the
August 2027 legal deadline, and to replace it with four smaller borough
jails (Brooklyn 2029, Bronx and Queens 2031, Manhattan 2032). The plan
cuts the jail population from about 6,600 to about 4,100, with $146
million next year for mental-health services in jails.
Sources: AP, USA Today, CNN (20 Nov 2026).

## Card 6 — Youth strikes across Europe

On Friday, tens of thousands of young people took to the streets in more
than 25 countries, demanding action on living costs, education and
housing and opposing military spending ("Money for schools, not war").
Italy's CGIL held a 24-hour general strike with demonstrations in more
than 100 cities; pupils walked out of more than 350 schools in Britain;
about 10,000 students were expected to rally in Cologne, Germany.
